## 💡 MVRV Model Evolution

### **Traditional MVRV Framework:**
```mathematical
MVRV = Market_Cap / Realized_Cap
MVRV_Z_Score = (Market_Cap - Realized_Cap) / σ(Market_Cap - Realized_Cap)
```

### **Post-ETF Adjustments:**
1. **Institutional Flow Integration**: ETF inflow/outflow impact on Realized Cap
2. **Custody Adjustments**: Exchange vs self-custody considerations
3. **Velocity Changes**: ETF impact on coin circulation patterns
4. **Liquidity Segmentation**: Available vs locked supply analysis

### **Enhanced Signal Interpretation:**
- **Traditional Levels**: MVRV Z-Score thresholds (historical)
- **ETF-Adjusted Levels**: Modified thresholds for institutional market
- **Combined Analysis**: Traditional + institutional indicator synthesis

## 🎯 LTH-SOPR Innovation

### **Long-Term Holder SOPR Framework:**
```mathematical
LTH_SOPR = Σ(LTH_Value_USD_when_spent) / Σ(LTH_Value_USD_when_created)
Age_Threshold = 155 days (minimum for LTH classification)
```

### **Post-ETF Considerations:**
- **Institutional Holders**: ETF shares vs direct Bitcoin holdings
- **Custody Patterns**: Exchange vs cold storage behavior
- **Profit-Taking Dynamics**: Institutional vs retail behavior differences
- **Market Impact**: Large LTH movements in institutional era
